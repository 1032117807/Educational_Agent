English Reading Practice: Main Idea and Detail Location

When answering a reading-comprehension question, first underline the key nouns,
verbs, dates, and contrast words in the question. Scan the article for the same
words or their synonyms. Read the sentence before and after the matched sentence
to confirm the meaning. Do not choose an answer only because it repeats a word
from the passage; check whether the relationship and scope match the question.

For a main-idea question, compare the first sentence of each paragraph and the
author's conclusion. A main idea describes the whole passage, while a detail
supports only one part. For a detail question, return to the paragraph that
contains the evidence and eliminate choices that add an unsupported cause,
number, or opinion.
